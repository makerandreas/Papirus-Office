## 2. LOKit seam before and after 12A (evidence)

`scripts/native-inventory.sh` (CI, LFS checkout, run `36730234004`) reported for both ABIs: `liblo-native-code.so` exporting `Java_org_libreoffice_kit_LibreOfficeKit_*`, `Java_org_libreoffice_kit_Office_*`, `Java_org_libreoffice_kit_Document_*`, `Java_org_libreoffice_android_Bootstrap_*`, `Java_com_sun_star_bridges_jni_1uno_*`, and **five MISSING** `Java_com_example_core_jni_LibreOfficeCore_native*` declarations.

| Before | After 12A |
|---|---|
| `LibreOfficeCore` declared 5 `external fun native*` under `com.example.core.jni`; none existed in the binary, so every call threw `UnsatisfiedLinkError` and dropped the engine to simulated mode | `LibreOfficeCore` declares **no** native methods; it loads the same soname chain and configures `TMPDIR`/`SAL_LOK_OPTIONS` through `org.libreoffice.kit.LibreOfficeKit.putenv` |
| no `org.libreoffice.kit` classes | `LibreOfficeKit` (4 methods), `Office` (7), `Document` (24) declare the exact symbol names the inventory script proves present |
| no native runtime | `LokitRuntime` bootstraps UNO once in `:office` (`initializeNative`, `getLibreOfficeKitHandle`, `bindMessageCallback`) and converts through `Document.saveAs(url, "pdf", "")` |
| no isolation | `OfficeEngineService` runs in `android:process=":office"`, single-threaded worker, 90 s caller timeout, 60 s idle `Process.killProcess` |
| `PagellaPdfCreator.convertDocumentToPdf` always used the text-only `InkyPdfExporter` fallback | LOKit conversion is attempted first and falls back to the existing path when unavailable or failed (unit and CI builds, where `LokitEngine.isNativeAvailable == false`) |

`LokitSubplan12aTest` pins the seam from the Kotlin side (reflection over declared native methods per class, no orphaned natives in `LibreOfficeCore`, a well-formed `fonts.conf` with the metric-compatible aliases, the manifest's `:office` registration, and simulated-mode safety). The inventory script now checks `org/libreoffice/kit/*.kt` instead of the deleted declarations.

### 3.2 Numbering paths the samples actually use

* **`<text:outline-style>` drives Sample-6's headings** (`BAB %1` / `%1.%2` / `%1.%2.%3`): its 45 `text:h` elements are top-level body children, never inside `<text:list>` (`lists containing text:h` = 0 for Samples 1, 2, 3, 5, 6).
* **Sample-4 is the list-wrapped contrast**: 14 of its 25 `text:h` sit inside `<text:list-item>`; `WWNum1` is `A`/`1`/`a` per level, and `continue-numbering="true"` links sibling lists, so the headings read `A. Latar Belakang`, `B. Rumusan Masalah`, `C. Tujuan`, then `A. Konsep Dasar Teori Kultivasi` … (`WWNum2`), `A. Kesimpulan` (`WWNum3`).
* **Suppression is a first-class case**: Sample-3, Sample-4 (headings) and Sample-6's `KATA PENGANTAR` / `DAFTAR ISI` / `DAFTAR PUSTAKA` declare `style:list-style-name=""`, and Sample-6's `WWNum21` carries `style:num-format=""`. Both must render with no prefix; 7A treats an empty declared name as "never numbered" and an empty `num-format` as an empty label.
* **Bullets are `text:list-level-style-bullet` with `text:bullet-char`** (`·`, `o`, `\uf0a7` in Sample-6's `WWNum3/8/10/16/17`), and the level's `style:name` points at a `ListLabel_*` text style that declares the label **font family** (`Aptos`) and no size of its own. The label-font fact is therefore a family, not a point size; an earlier reading of this audit claimed 10 pt and was wrong (the sizes in `styles.xml` are the heading character styles: 20/16/14 pt).

### 3.3 Labels asserted in `OdtListNumberingTest`

Sample-6 (normalised; zero-width joiners and authored line breaks collapse to single spaces): `BAB 1 PENDAHULUAN`, `1.1 Latar Belakang`, `1.2 Rumusan Masalah`, `BAB 2 PEMBAHASAN`, `2.1.1 Definisi dan Etimologi Talenta`, `BAB 3 PENUTUP`, `3.1 Kesimpulan`, `3.2 Saran`, `3.2.1 Bagi Siswa dan Pemuda Kristen`, with `KATA PENGANTAR`, `DAFTAR ISI`, `DAFTAR PUSTAKA` unnumbered. Ordered list labels: `1.` to `10.` in Rumusan Masalah and the other `WWNum*` lists, plus a bare form (`1` to `4`, no `num-suffix`) authored by `WWNum14`. Sample-6 has no alpha or roman list otherwise, so the alpha and roman renderings are asserted on the counter model and on Sample-4's `WWNum1` (`A`/`1`/`a` per level) instead. Bullet labels keep the middle dot without doubling the `num-suffix`. Sample-4: `A.`/`B.`/`C.` as listed above.
