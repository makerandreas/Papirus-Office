## 2. Sample-5 source of truth (parsed ODT, not Word)

From `tests/inky/Sample-5.odt` `styles.xml` / `content.xml`:

- `style:default-style` paragraph/text: Aptos 12pt
- Normal: Times New Roman 12pt
- Judul1 parent Normal: `fo:font-size=14pt` + bold (not Word’s 20pt Aptos Display Char)
- Judul2 parent Normal: bold only → inherit 12pt
- No character style named Tabel1. Char styles are T17 (empty text-properties) and T18+ (`fo:font-weight=bold`). Headings are `text:p` auto-styles (`P36` etc.) parenting Judul1.

D2 unit test is therefore synthetic XML: empty `Tabel1` is not bold; `TBold` with `fo:font-weight=bold` is.
